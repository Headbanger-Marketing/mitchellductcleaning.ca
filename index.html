FAQs

Straight Answers, No Guesswork

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

How often do Mitchell homes need the ducts cleaned?

Out toward the fields, two years is the working rhythm, and in-town older stock the same. Newer tight builds can stretch to three. When the return grilles grow felt ahead of schedule, the schedule shortens.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified, insured, and able to show you both before the visit. The province does not designate this trade, so a trained crew and full coverage are the credentials doing the work, and ours carry them on every job.

What evidence do we get at the end?

Photographs of your duct interiors taken before the cleaning and after it, reviewed with you before we leave or emailed that evening. They are your photos, of your house.

The dryer runs hot and long. Is it worth replacing?

Replace the vent restriction first. We brush the pipe to the outdoor hood, read the airflow, and in most Mitchell homes the dryer goes back to single cycles. If the numbers say otherwise, we say so.

Describe a full visit.

Collector on and the system negative, registers washed, runs brushed one by one, blower compartment opened and cleaned, photos taken and reviewed. Town houses run about three hours, farm properties with additions longer.

Can the woodstove smell ever come out of the vents?

Mostly, yes. The residue is what holds the smell, cleaning removes the bulk, and the sanitizer pass on the clean metal addresses the remainder. Woodstove houses are routine here.

Dusting daily no matter the season, why?

Leaking return ducts in the cellar resupply the system faster than any cleaning empties it. The pressure test locates the leaks, the sealing closes them, and the daily dusting ends.

Do you go outside Mitchell?

Sebringville, Dublin, Kirkton, St. Marys, Listowel, Shakespeare, and the farm places throughout Perth. Rural addresses welcome, just give us the concession.

Do you serve areas outside of Mitchell, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Mitchell and nearby communities including Sebringville, St. Marys, Listowel, Dublin, and the surrounding area.
